## 2026-08-16 — Floating Brush Toolbar

The desktop editor view at 1280×720 renders the floating brush toolbar in the canvas frame’s upper-left corner. It is visually distinct from the worksheet, does not overlap the history controls, and exposes both size and opacity sliders with visible values.

The phone view at 390×844 renders a reduced-width version of the same toolbar. Its controls remain legible, the full worksheet remains visible, and it does not collide with the undo/redo controls or bottom brush dock.
